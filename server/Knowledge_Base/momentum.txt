# Momentum Trading - Complete Strategy Guide

## What is Momentum Trading?
Momentum trading is an investment strategy that capitalizes on the continuation of existing trends in the market. It operates on the principle that assets in motion tend to stay in motion, allowing traders to profit from sustained price movements.

## Core Principles

### Trend Continuation
- **Uptrend**: Buy assets moving strongly upward
- **Downtrend**: Sell short assets moving strongly downward
- **Sideways**: Avoid assets with no clear direction
- **Strength**: Focus on assets with strong momentum

### Price Action Analysis
- **Breakouts**: Price breaking through key levels
- **Volume Confirmation**: Higher volume on momentum moves
- **Support/Resistance**: Key levels for entry/exit
- **Chart Patterns**: Triangles, flags, pennants

## Momentum Indicators

### Rate of Change (ROC)
- **Calculation**: (Current Price - Price n periods ago) / Price n periods ago × 100
- **Interpretation**: Positive = upward momentum, Negative = downward momentum
- **Timeframes**: 5, 10, 20 periods commonly used
- **Signals**: ROC above zero = bullish, below zero = bearish

### Momentum Oscillator
- **Calculation**: Current Price - Price n periods ago
- **Interpretation**: Higher values = stronger momentum
- **Divergence**: Price vs momentum divergence signals
- **Zero Line**: Crossovers indicate momentum changes

### Commodity Channel Index (CCI)
- **Calculation**: (Typical Price - SMA) / (0.015 × Mean Deviation)
- **Range**: -100 to +100 typically
- **Overbought**: Above +100
- **Oversold**: Below -100
- **Signals**: CCI crossovers and divergences

### Williams %R
- **Calculation**: (Highest High - Current Close) / (Highest High - Lowest Low) × -100
- **Range**: 0 to -100
- **Overbought**: Above -20
- **Oversold**: Below -80
- **Signals**: Crossovers and divergences

## Momentum Trading Strategies

### Breakout Strategy
1. **Identify**: Key resistance/support levels
2. **Wait**: For price to break through level
3. **Confirm**: With volume increase
4. **Enter**: In direction of breakout
5. **Exit**: When momentum weakens

### Pullback Strategy
1. **Identify**: Strong trending asset
2. **Wait**: For pullback to support/resistance
3. **Enter**: When price bounces off level
4. **Confirm**: With momentum indicators
5. **Exit**: When trend reverses

### Momentum Divergence Strategy
1. **Identify**: Price vs momentum divergence
2. **Wait**: For momentum confirmation
3. **Enter**: In direction of momentum
4. **Confirm**: With volume and other indicators
5. **Exit**: When divergence resolves

## Entry and Exit Rules

### Entry Signals
- **Breakout**: Price breaks through key level with volume
- **Pullback**: Price bounces off support/resistance
- **Momentum**: Indicators show strong momentum
- **Volume**: Higher than average volume
- **Trend**: Align with overall market trend

### Exit Signals
- **Momentum Loss**: Indicators show weakening momentum
- **Volume Decline**: Volume decreases significantly
- **Support/Resistance**: Price hits key level
- **Divergence**: Price vs momentum divergence
- **Trend Change**: Overall trend reverses

### Stop Loss Placement
- **Below Support**: For long positions
- **Above Resistance**: For short positions
- **ATR Based**: 2-3 times Average True Range
- **Percentage**: 2-5% of entry price
- **Technical**: Below/above key technical levels

## Risk Management

### Position Sizing
- **Risk Per Trade**: 1-2% of account
- **Maximum Risk**: 5% of account at any time
- **Correlation**: Avoid highly correlated positions
- **Diversification**: Spread risk across sectors

### Portfolio Management
- **Maximum Positions**: 5-10 positions
- **Sector Limits**: Max 30% in one sector
- **Market Cap**: Mix of large, mid, small cap
- **Geographic**: Diversify across regions

### Risk Controls
- **Daily Loss Limit**: Stop trading after 3% loss
- **Weekly Loss Limit**: Stop trading after 5% loss
- **Monthly Review**: Assess strategy performance
- **Drawdown Limits**: Maximum 10% drawdown

## Market Conditions

### Trending Markets
- **Best Strategy**: Trend following
- **Indicators**: Moving averages, MACD
- **Timeframes**: Longer timeframes work better
- **Risk**: Lower risk, higher probability

### Ranging Markets
- **Best Strategy**: Mean reversion
- **Indicators**: RSI, Bollinger Bands
- **Timeframes**: Shorter timeframes work better
- **Risk**: Higher risk, lower probability

### Volatile Markets
- **Best Strategy**: Breakout trading
- **Indicators**: Volume, volatility indicators
- **Timeframes**: Medium timeframes
- **Risk**: High risk, high reward

## Common Mistakes

### Over-trading
- **Mistake**: Trading too frequently
- **Reality**: Quality over quantity
- **Solution**: Wait for high-probability setups

### Ignoring Risk
- **Mistake**: Not using stop losses
- **Reality**: Risk management is crucial
- **Solution**: Always use stop losses

### Chasing Performance
- **Mistake**: Following hot stocks
- **Reality**: Past performance doesn't guarantee future
- **Solution**: Focus on strategy, not performance

### Emotional Trading
- **Mistake**: Letting emotions drive decisions
- **Reality**: Emotions lead to poor decisions
- **Solution**: Stick to your plan

## Advanced Techniques

- **Confirmation**: Align signals across timeframes
- **Risk**: Higher timeframe determines risk

### Sector Rotation
- **Identify**: Leading sectors
- **Focus**: Trade strongest sectors
- **Rotate**: Move to new leaders
- **Diversify**: Don't put all eggs in one basket
