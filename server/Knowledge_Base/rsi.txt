# Relative Strength Index (RSI) - Complete Technical Analysis Guide

## What is RSI?
The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum oscillator that measures the speed and magnitude of price changes. It oscillates between 0 and 100, helping identify overbought and oversold conditions.

## RSI Calculation
RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + RS))
Where RS = Average Gain / Average Loss

### Step-by-Step Calculation
1. Calculate price changes for each period
2. Separate gains and losses
3. Calculate average gain and average loss over 14 periods
4. Calculate Relative Strength (RS) = Average Gain / Average Loss
5. Calculate RSI = 100 - (100 / (1 + RS))

## Standard RSI Settings
- **Default Period**: 14 days
- **Overbought Level**: 70
- **Oversold Level**: 30
- **Neutral Level**: 50

## RSI Interpretation

### Overbought Conditions
- **RSI > 70**: Potentially overbought
- **RSI > 80**: Strongly overbought
- **RSI > 90**: Extremely overbought
- **Significance**: Price may be due for a pullback

### Oversold Conditions
- **RSI < 30**: Potentially oversold
- **RSI < 20**: Strongly oversold
- **RSI < 10**: Extremely oversold
- **Significance**: Price may be due for a bounce

### Neutral Conditions
- **RSI 40-60**: Neutral zone
- **RSI around 50**: No clear momentum
- **Significance**: Wait for clearer signals

## RSI Trading Signals

### Basic Signals
- **Buy Signal**: RSI crosses above 30 (oversold recovery)
- **Sell Signal**: RSI crosses below 70 (overbought decline)
- **Strong Buy**: RSI below 20 then crosses above 20
- **Strong Sell**: RSI above 80 then crosses below 80

### Divergence Signals

#### Bullish Divergence
- **Price**: Makes lower lows
- **RSI**: Makes higher lows
- **Significance**: Potential bullish reversal
- **Confirmation**: RSI breaks above 50

#### Bearish Divergence
- **Price**: Makes higher highs
- **RSI**: Makes lower highs
- **Significance**: Potential bearish reversal
- **Confirmation**: RSI breaks below 50

### Hidden Divergence

#### Hidden Bullish Divergence
- **Price**: Makes higher lows
- **RSI**: Makes lower lows
- **Significance**: Uptrend continuation
- **Use**: Buy on pullbacks in uptrend

#### Hidden Bearish Divergence
- **Price**: Makes lower highs
- **RSI**: Makes higher highs
- **Significance**: Downtrend continuation
- **Use**: Sell on rallies in downtrend

## Advanced RSI Techniques

### RSI Failure Swings
- **Bullish Failure Swing**: RSI below 30, rises above 30, pulls back but stays above 30, then rises again
- **Bearish Failure Swing**: RSI above 70, falls below 70, bounces but stays below 70, then falls again
- **Significance**: Strong reversal signals

### RSI Range Shifts
- **Bull Market Range**: RSI typically 40-80
- **Bear Market Range**: RSI typically 20-60
- **Significance**: Indicates overall market trend

### RSI Centerline Crossovers
- **Bullish**: RSI crosses above 50
- **Bearish**: RSI crosses below 50
- **Significance**: Momentum shift confirmation

## RSI Timeframes

### Short-term (1-5 days)
- **Period**: 5-7
- **Overbought**: 80
- **Oversold**: 20
- **Use**: Day trading, scalping

### Medium-term (1-4 weeks)
- **Period**: 14 (default)
- **Overbought**: 70
- **Oversold**: 30
- **Use**: Swing trading

### Long-term (1-3 months)
- **Period**: 21-30
- **Overbought**: 75
- **Oversold**: 25
- **Use**: Position trading

## RSI Trading Strategies

### Mean Reversion Strategy
1. **Entry**: RSI below 30 (oversold)
2. **Exit**: RSI above 70 (overbought)
3. **Stop Loss**: Below recent low
4. **Best For**: Ranging markets

### Trend Following Strategy
1. **Entry**: RSI above 50 in uptrend
2. **Exit**: RSI below 50 in downtrend
3. **Stop Loss**: Below trend line
4. **Best For**: Trending markets

### Divergence Strategy
1. **Identify**: Price vs RSI divergence
2. **Entry**: Wait for RSI confirmation
3. **Exit**: Opposite divergence or target
4. **Stop Loss**: Beyond divergence point

## RSI with Other Indicators

### RSI + Moving Averages
- **Trend Confirmation**: Use MA for trend direction
- **RSI Signals**: Use RSI for entry/exit timing
- **Combination**: MA trend + RSI momentum

### RSI + MACD
- **Momentum Confirmation**: Both show momentum
- **Divergence Analysis**: Compare both indicators
- **Signal Strength**: Stronger when both align

### RSI + Volume
- **Volume Confirmation**: Higher volume on RSI signals
- **Breakout Confirmation**: Volume + RSI breakout
- **Reversal Confirmation**: Volume + RSI divergence

## Common RSI Mistakes

### Overbought/Oversold Misinterpretation
- **Mistake**: Always selling at 70, buying at 30
- **Reality**: RSI can stay overbought/oversold for extended periods
- **Solution**: Wait for confirmation signals

### Ignoring Trend Context
- **Mistake**: Using same levels in all market conditions
- **Reality**: RSI levels vary by market trend
- **Solution**: Adjust levels based on market context

### Single Indicator Reliance
- **Mistake**: Using only RSI for trading decisions
- **Reality**: RSI works best with other indicators
- **Solution**: Combine with trend and volume analysis

## RSI Optimization

### Period Optimization
- **Shorter Periods**: More sensitive, more signals
- **Longer Periods**: Less sensitive, fewer false signals
- **Best Practice**: Test different periods for your timeframe

### Level Optimization
- **Tight Levels**: 60/40 for more signals
- **Wide Levels**: 80/20 for fewer, stronger signals
- **Adaptive Levels**: Adjust based on volatility

### Market-Specific Settings
- **Volatile Markets**: Use wider levels (75/25)
- **Quiet Markets**: Use tighter levels (65/35)
- **Trending Markets**: Use trend-following approach

## RSI Patterns

### RSI Double Bottom
- **Appearance**: RSI forms two lows at similar levels
- **Significance**: Strong bullish reversal signal
- **Confirmation**: RSI breaks above 50

### RSI Double Top
- **Appearance**: RSI forms two highs at similar levels
- **Significance**: Strong bearish reversal signal
- **Confirmation**: RSI breaks below 50

### RSI Triangle
- **Appearance**: RSI forms converging trend lines
- **Significance**: Impending breakout
- **Direction**: Breakout direction indicates trend

## Practical Tips

### RSI Best Practices
1. **Use Multiple Timeframes**: Confirm signals across timeframes
2. **Combine with Price Action**: Look for candlestick patterns
3. **Consider Market Context**: Adjust strategy based on market conditions
4. **Practice Risk Management**: Always use stop losses

### RSI Limitations
- **Lagging Indicator**: RSI follows price, doesn't predict
- **Whipsaws**: False signals in choppy markets
- **Trending Markets**: RSI can stay overbought/oversold
- **News Events**: RSI may not reflect fundamental changes

### RSI Success Factors
- **Trend Alignment**: RSI signals work best with trend
- **Volume Confirmation**: Higher volume on RSI signals
- **Multiple Confirmation**: Use with other indicators
- **Proper Risk Management**: Always use stop losses

Remember: RSI is a momentum indicator, not a trend indicator. It works best when combined with trend analysis and proper risk management. Always consider the overall market context when interpreting RSI signals.
