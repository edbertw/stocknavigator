# Correlation Trading - Complete Strategy Guide

## What is Correlation Trading?
Correlation trading is a strategy that exploits the relationship between different assets, indices, or securities. It involves trading the correlation itself rather than the individual assets, capitalizing on when correlations deviate from their historical norms.

## Understanding Correlation

### Correlation Coefficient
- **Range**: -1 to +1
- **+1**: Perfect positive correlation (move together)
- **-1**: Perfect negative correlation (move opposite)
- **0**: No correlation (independent movement)
- **Calculation**: Covariance / (Standard Deviation A × Standard Deviation B)

### Types of Correlation
- **Positive Correlation**: Assets move in same direction
- **Negative Correlation**: Assets move in opposite directions
- **Zero Correlation**: No relationship between movements
- **Dynamic Correlation**: Changes over time

## Correlation Trading Strategies

### Pairs Trading
- **Concept**: Trade two correlated assets
- **Long Position**: Buy underperforming asset
- **Short Position**: Sell outperforming asset
- **Profit**: When correlation returns to normal
- **Risk**: Correlation may not return

### Index vs Individual Stocks
- **Strategy**: Trade correlation between index and stocks
- **Long Correlation**: Buy index, sell individual stocks
- **Short Correlation**: Sell index, buy individual stocks
- **Timing**: Enter when correlation deviates

### Sector Rotation
- **Concept**: Trade correlation between sectors
- **Leading Sectors**: Buy when correlation increases
- **Lagging Sectors**: Sell when correlation decreases
- **Timing**: Enter during correlation shifts

## Correlation Analysis Tools

### Rolling Correlation
- **Calculation**: Correlation over rolling window
- **Window**: 20, 50, 100 periods commonly used
- **Purpose**: Shows how correlation changes over time
- **Signals**: Extreme values indicate opportunities

### Correlation Matrix
- **Purpose**: Shows all pairwise correlations
- **Use**: Identify strongest/weakest relationships
- **Analysis**: Find trading opportunities
- **Updates**: Recalculate regularly

### Correlation Heatmap
- **Visualization**: Color-coded correlation matrix
- **Colors**: Red (negative), Green (positive)
- **Intensity**: Darker colors = stronger correlation
- **Analysis**: Quick visual identification

## Trading Signals

### Correlation Breakout
- **Signal**: Correlation breaks historical range
- **Long**: When correlation increases significantly
- **Short**: When correlation decreases significantly
- **Confirmation**: Volume and other indicators

### Mean Reversion
- **Signal**: Correlation deviates from mean
- **Entry**: When correlation is extreme
- **Exit**: When correlation returns to mean
- **Risk**: Correlation may not revert

### Trend Following
- **Signal**: Correlation trend continues
- **Entry**: In direction of correlation trend
- **Exit**: When trend reverses
- **Risk**: Trend may reverse suddenly

## Risk Management

### Correlation Risk
- **Definition**: Risk that correlation changes
- **Mitigation**: Diversify across uncorrelated assets
- **Monitoring**: Track correlation changes
- **Adjustment**: Modify positions as needed

### Liquidity Risk
- **Definition**: Risk of not being able to exit
- **Mitigation**: Trade liquid instruments
- **Monitoring**: Track bid-ask spreads
- **Adjustment**: Reduce position sizes

### Model Risk
- **Definition**: Risk of model being wrong
- **Mitigation**: Use multiple models
- **Monitoring**: Track model performance
- **Adjustment**: Update models regularly

## Market Conditions

### High Correlation Periods
- **Characteristics**: All assets move together
- **Strategy**: Focus on relative strength
- **Risk**: Diversification less effective
- **Opportunity**: Sector rotation strategies

### Low Correlation Periods
- **Characteristics**: Assets move independently
- **Strategy**: Focus on individual analysis
- **Risk**: Harder to predict movements
- **Opportunity**: Stock picking strategies

### Volatile Periods
- **Characteristics**: High volatility, changing correlations
- **Strategy**: Dynamic correlation trading
- **Risk**: Correlations can change quickly
- **Opportunity**: Short-term trading opportunities

## Common Mistakes

### Over-reliance on Historical Correlation
- **Mistake**: Assuming past correlation continues
- **Reality**: Correlations change over time
- **Solution**: Use dynamic correlation analysis

### Ignoring Market Context
- **Mistake**: Trading correlation without context
- **Reality**: Market conditions affect correlations
- **Solution**: Consider overall market environment

### Poor Risk Management
- **Mistake**: Not managing correlation risk
- **Reality**: Correlations can change suddenly
- **Solution**: Use proper risk management

### Over-optimization
- **Mistake**: Over-fitting to historical data
- **Reality**: Past performance doesn't guarantee future
- **Solution**: Use out-of-sample testing

## Advanced Techniques

### Dynamic Correlation
- **Concept**: Correlation that changes over time
- **Calculation**: Using rolling windows
- **Analysis**: Identify correlation regimes
- **Trading**: Adapt strategy to regime

### Correlation Clustering
- **Concept**: Group assets by correlation
- **Method**: Hierarchical clustering
- **Use**: Portfolio construction
- **Benefit**: Better diversification

### Correlation Regime Detection
- **Concept**: Identify different correlation regimes
- **Method**: Statistical tests for regime changes
- **Use**: Adapt strategy to regime
- **Benefit**: Better risk management

## Performance Metrics

### Correlation Metrics
- **Average Correlation**: Mean correlation over period
- **Correlation Volatility**: Standard deviation of correlation
- **Correlation Skewness**: Asymmetry of correlation distribution
- **Correlation Kurtosis**: Tail thickness of correlation distribution

### Trading Metrics
- **Sharpe Ratio**: Risk-adjusted returns
- **Maximum Drawdown**: Largest peak-to-trough decline
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of profitable trades
- **Profit Factor**: Gross profit / Gross loss

## Best Practices

### Research and Analysis
- **Data Quality**: Use clean, reliable data
- **Multiple Timeframes**: Analyze different periods
- **Statistical Tests**: Validate correlation significance
- **Out-of-Sample Testing**: Test on unseen data

### Risk Management
- **Position Sizing**: Size positions appropriately
- **Diversification**: Don't rely on single correlation
- **Monitoring**: Track correlation changes
- **Adjustment**: Modify strategy as needed

### Execution
- **Timing**: Enter at optimal correlation levels
- **Confirmation**: Use multiple signals
- **Patience**: Wait for high-probability setups
- **Discipline**: Stick to your plan

Remember: Correlation trading requires sophisticated analysis and risk management. Correlations can change quickly, especially during market stress. Always use proper risk management and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
