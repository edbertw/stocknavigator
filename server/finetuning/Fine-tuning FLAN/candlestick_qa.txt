"Question","Answer"  
"What is a candlestick chart?","A candlestick chart (also called Japanese candlestick chart or K-line) is a style of financial chart used to describe price movements of a security, derivative, or currency."  
"How does a candlestick chart differ from a bar chart?","While similar in appearance, each candlestick represents four key pieces of information: open, close (thick body), high, and low (wick). Bar charts display the same data but in a different visual format."  
"What information does a single candlestick represent?","1. Open & Close (body), 2. High & Low (wicks/shadows). The color/fill indicates whether the price rose or fell during the period."  
"Who developed candlestick charts?","They were thought to be developed in the 18th century by Munehisa Homma, a Japanese rice trader, but evidence suggests they likely emerged in the late 1800s during Japan’s Meiji period."  
"How were candlestick charts introduced to the Western world?","Steve Nison popularized them in his 1991 book *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques*."  
"What is the 'real body' in a candlestick?","The area between the open and close prices. Hollow/green means close > open (price rose); filled/red means close < open (price fell)."  
"What do the wicks (shadows) represent?","The highest and lowest traded prices during the candlestick’s time frame."  
"How is the price range of a candlestick calculated?","Range = High price − Low price."  
"How do modern charting tools customize candlesticks?","Users can modify colors, body/wick styles, and even adjust candle width based on volume."  
"What is a hollow candlestick chart?","A variation where color and fill distinguish price relationships (e.g., hollow = price rise, filled = price drop)."  
"Can a candlestick lack a body or wick?","Yes. A missing body means open = close; missing wicks mean high/low = open/close."  
"What does a long candlestick body indicate?","Strong buying or selling pressure, reflecting intense trading activity."  
"How do candlesticks help traders?","They visually convey price action, trends, and potential reversals by showing opening/closing prices and volatility (via wicks)."  
"What is a candlestick pattern?","A specific sequence of candlesticks used to identify trends or reversals (e.g., Doji, Hammer, Engulfing)."  
"What is Heikin-Ashi?","A modified candlestick type using averaged prices: Close = (Open+High+Low+Close)/4, Open = (Prior Open + Prior Close)/2. It smooths price noise."  
"How does Heikin-Ashi differ from regular candlesticks?","Heikin-Ashi candles may not reflect actual open/close prices, and long wicks often indicate stronger trends."  
"Can candlesticks be volume-based?","Yes. Some charts use open/high/low/close values for a set volume range (e.g., per 100,000 shares)."  
"Which markets use candlestick charts?","Stocks, forex, commodities, and options—primarily for technical analysis."  
"What does a white/green candle with a long body signify?","Strong bullish sentiment (buyers dominated the period)."  
"What does a black/red candle with a long body indicate?","Strong bearish sentiment (sellers dominated the period)."  
"Why are candlestick charts popular in technical analysis?","They densely pack price data, making trends, reversals, and volatility easy to interpret visually."  
"Do candlesticks show real-time price movements?","Yes, as they form, they update to reflect current price action within the selected timeframe."  
"Are candlestick patterns reliable alone?","No, they should be combined with other indicators (e.g., moving averages, RSI) for confirmation."  
"Who disputed Homma’s role in candlestick creation?","Steve Nison noted in *Beyond Candlesticks* that Homma likely didn’t use them; they emerged later in the 1800s."  
"How do wicks indicate market sentiment?","Long upper wicks suggest rejection of higher prices (selling pressure); long lower wicks indicate buying interest at lows."
